Install MazTV on Your Device

Choose the device you use. We’ll show you the recommended player, where to get it, and how to connect your MazTV subscription.

Before you begin — one important distinction

The player app and your MazTV subscription are separate. Your welcome message must provide a login method supported by the player you choose (for example, Xtream Codes or an M3U/M3U8 playlist). If your MazTV welcome message tells you to install a specific MazTV-branded app, follow that message instead. Do not pay a third-party app activation fee unless you have checked that the app supports your login.

Have your MazTV login ready

Use the exact server address, username, password or playlist link supplied in your MazTV welcome message. These details are case-sensitive in some apps. Never post them publicly or send them to an unverified person.

Still need help?

Send us the device brand and model, the app you installed, and the step where you got stuck. Do not send your password or full playlist URL.
