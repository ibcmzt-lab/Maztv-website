What Is Streaming TV?

A simpler, more flexible way to enjoy television using the internet.

🌐

TV Through the Internet

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Instead of arriving through a traditional cable connection or satellite dish, television programming is delivered over an internet connection.

📺

Watch on Your Devices

Depending on the service and compatible apps, you can watch on a Smart TV, streaming stick, streaming box, phone, tablet or computer.

🎬

Live TV and On-Demand

Streaming services may offer live channels, sports, movies, series and on-demand libraries. What is included depends on the specific service and subscription.

📶

Your Internet Connection Matters

A stable internet connection helps provide a smoother viewing experience. Wi-Fi quality, device capability and network congestion can all affect playback.

Is IPTV Legal?

Yes. IPTV is a legitimate technology for delivering television over the internet. As with any TV service, the important question is whether the provider is authorized to distribute the content it offers.

✅

The Technology Is Legal

Streaming television over the internet is simply a method of delivering video. Legitimate broadcasters and streaming platforms use internet-based delivery every day.

🛡️

Content Rights Matter

A service should have the necessary rights or permissions for the channels, films, sports and other copyrighted content it distributes. The technology alone does not determine whether a particular service is authorized.

🔎

Choose With Confidence

Before subscribing, ask who provides the service, what is included, how support works and whether the provider is authorized to offer the content in your location.

💬

Questions? Ask Us

Not sure what you need to get started? Contact our team for help understanding device compatibility, setup options and the details of your service.

General information only, not legal advice. The legality of a particular service depends on the content offered, the provider's rights and the laws that apply in your location.
