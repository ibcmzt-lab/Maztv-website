Easy Smart TV & Device Setup

Get up and running in under 3 minutes on any Smart TV or Streaming Device.

Firestick / Android TV

Best app experience using TiviMate or XCIPTV.

  1. Download TiviMate or XCIPTV.
  2. Select Xtream Codes Login.
  3. Enter your trial credentials and server URL.

Samsung Smart TV

Direct install from Samsung Tizen App Store.

  1. Open Samsung App Store on your TV.
  2. Search and install IPTV Smarters Pro or IBO Player.
  3. Enter portal link: http://p.maztv.xyz:8080

LG Smart TV

Direct install from LG Content Store (webOS).

  1. Open LG Content Store on TV screen.
  2. Install IBO Player or IPTV Smarters.
  3. Load your playlist using your Xtream credentials.
